# MecAgent: AI CAD Copilot

Sources:
- https://mecagent.com/?ref=nextaitool.com
- https://mecagent.com/pricing-pro
- https://mecagent.com/terms

- Product focus: an AI assistant for mechanical CAD automation, emphasizing repetitive task reduction and custom automation creation without coding.
- Target platforms: advertised support for SolidWorks, Inventor, Onshape, Fusion 360, NX, Solid Edge, Creo, and planned CATIA support.
- Pricing tiers: Starter ($84/mo, 200 credits, 50 saved automations, 10 min execution), Advanced+ ($417/mo, 1500 credits, 400 saved automations, unlimited execution), and custom Enterprise with on-prem/private cloud, fine-tuning, unlimited credits, and SOC 2 compliance.
- Security angle: terms describe account registration, data processing, and a Privacy Mode that avoids storing customer data or using it for training.
- Useful takeaways: commercial CAD AI products need strong workflow integration, credit-based execution limits, enterprise data controls, and clear support for real CAD packages.
- Product lesson: for a FeatureScript CAD assistant, a viable business model may combine a low-touch subscription tier with paid premium/enterprise capabilities.
