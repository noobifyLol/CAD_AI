# FeatureScript Resource Collections

Sources:
- https://www.cadsharp.com/featurescripts/
- https://www.frcdesign.org/resources/featurescripts/
- https://github.com/dcowden/featurescript

- CadSharp library: large curated collection of Onshape FeatureScript custom features, including Boolean groups, constrained surfaces, curve generators, pattern/sweep tools, measurement utilities, and manufacturing helpers.
- FRCDesign list: community-curated FeatureScript bundles and recommended tools for FRC-style design, such as origin references, belt and chain generators, lightening, fillet automation, and specialized manufacturing utilities.
- dcowden/featurescript repo: an "Awesome FeatureScripts" catalog organized by joinery, surfaces, manufacturing, primitives, structures, fasteners, patterning, sheet metal, analysis, and other real-world tools.
- Data use: these resources are valuable for building a FeatureScript knowledge base, extracting API patterns, and assembling concrete examples for training or retrieval.
- AI lesson: real FeatureScript workflows emphasize reusable custom features, strong query-based selection, and domain-specific utilities rather than generic JavaScript-like constructs.
