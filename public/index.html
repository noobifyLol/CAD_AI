How to use this tool

Step 1 — Generate FeatureScript
Type a description of your part in the Generate tab. Click Generate. Copy the code.

Step 2 — Paste into Onshape Feature Studio
In Onshape, open your document → create or open a Feature Studio tab. Select all (Ctrl+A) and paste your code.

Step 3 — Insert the Feature
Switch to your Part Studio. The feature now appears in the feature toolbar. Click it select a plane (Top, Front, Right, or a face) click . Your geometry appears instantly.

Got an error?
Copy the FeatureScript and the error text from Onshape. Paste both into the Debug tab → click Fix It copy the fixed code.

Have a picture of a part?
Upload it in the Analyze Image tab. The AI will describe the part and generate FeatureScript for it automatically.
