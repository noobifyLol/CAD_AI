How to use

Step 1 — Generate FeatureScript

Type a description of your part in the Generate tab. Include shape, dimensions, holes, and any fillets. Click Generate or press Ctrl+Enter.

Step 2 — Paste into Onshape

In Onshape, open your document and create or open a Feature Studio tab. Select all (Ctrl+A) and paste the generated code.

Step 3 — Insert the feature

Switch to your Part Studio. The feature appears in the toolbar. Click it, select a plane (Top, Front, Right, or any flat face), then click the checkmark. The geometry appears on the selected plane.

Got an error?

Use the Debug tab. Paste your FeatureScript and the error message from Onshape. The AI will explain what went wrong and return a corrected version.

Have a photo of a part?

Use the Image tab. Upload any photo — a real part, a CAD screenshot, or a hand sketch. The AI will describe it and generate FeatureScript automatically. You can add extra instructions to override specific dimensions.

Supported shapes

Box, cube, block — Cylinder, rod, shaft, pin — Flat plate — Polygon (triangle, hex, any N-sided) — Linkage arm, connecting rod, crank arm — Mounting plate with holes — L-bracket, angle bracket — Pipe flange with bolt circle — Hex nut — Washer — Bushing, sleeve

Dimension tips

Dimensions default to inches. You can say mm and the AI converts automatically. For cylinders, say diameter or radius. For hex shapes, say across flats. For hollow parts (bushing, washer), specify OD and ID. Missing dimensions default to something sensible.
