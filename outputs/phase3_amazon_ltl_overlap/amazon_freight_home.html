Frequently asked questions

Amazon Freight is a connection point for businesses to move freight via the Amazon transportation network. With 80,000+ trailers, 24,000+ intermodal containers, and advanced logistics technology, shippers can access the same capabilities that move Amazon packages safely, quickly, and reliably. Amazon Freight is an asset-backed freight broker licensed under MC-826094 (US DOT: 2881058).

Amazon Freight was built by a shipper, for shippers. The service leverages Amazon’s own logistics infrastructure, which includes 80,000+ Amazon-owned trailers and 50,000+ vetted carriers. This provides shippers with reliable capacity during peak seasons and market shortages. Amazon’s logistics technology optimizes shipment routing and performance, and all services are accessible through a self-service online portal.

Creating an Amazon Freight account takes a few steps. Go here to create login credentials and enter basic business information. Begin quoting and booking loads immediately after account creation.

Once an account is created, shippers can begin quoting shipments immediately. Create an account to get started. The self-service portal allows booking, tracking, and managing shipments on demand.

Amazon Freight offers three core shipping services—full truckload (FTL), less-than-truckload (LTL), and intermodal—both inbound to Amazon facilities and to third-party destinations.

All services include 24/7 support and booking through the online portal .

Amazon Freight provides businesses with access to the Amazon transportation network, including:

  • 80,000+ Amazon-owned trailers
  • 50,000+ vetted carriers
  • 24,000+ intermodal containers
  • Self-service online portal for booking and tracking shipments
  • 24/7 operations team support
  • Real-time GPS tracking on all Amazon trailers

Amazon Freight ships coast-to-coast across the continental United States. The network covers inbound shipments to Amazon fulfilment centers, shipper facilities, and third-party facilities. View coverage maps for FTL, LTL, and Intermodal services. For lanes not shown in the network, contact the team for further options.

Pricing is tailored to specific shipping needs and volume:

  • Small and medium businesses: Create an account for instant quotes and transparent lane pricing.
  • Enterprise shippers: Connect with the sales team for customized pricing based on volume and requirements.

The self-service online portal provides on-demand shipment management. Amazon trailers are equipped with GPS sensors for real-time tracking through the Amazon Freight account.
